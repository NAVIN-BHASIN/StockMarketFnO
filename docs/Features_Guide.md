# Project Features Guide
## HFT Decision Terminal & AI Intelligence Engine

### 1. Global Markets Section
- **Live Indices:** Tracking NIFTY, BANKNIFTY, FINNIFTY, and Major Global Indices.
- **Market Breadth:** Real-time Advances/Declines for NSE and BSE.
- **News Terminal:** Triple-tabbed news for Indian, Global, and Geopolitical impact.

### 2. HFT Decision Engine
- **AI Predictions:** 3-tier target forecasting (Intraday, Weekly, Monthly).
- **Conviction Scoring:** 0-8 scale scoring for every trade setup.
- **Historical Backtesting:** Automated win-rate and profit analysis for any symbol.
- **Live 40-Day Trend:** Combined Price and OI visual trend charts.

### 3. AI Stocks Screener (NEW)
- **Momentum Scanner:** One-click scan of the entire 180+ FnO universe.
- **Breakout Detection:** Real-time identification of institutional volume spikes.
- **Winning Probability:** AI-scored probability of trade success.
- **Technical Signals:** Automated RSI, EMA, and MACD trend categorization.

### 4. Advanced Options Intelligence
- **Deep-Dive Search:** Granular analysis of any Strike Price and Expiry.
- **Strike PCR:** Support/Resistance identification via local PCR.
- **Strategy Feasibility:** Recommendations for Spreads (Bull Call, Bear Put, etc.).

### 5. Multi-Timeframe Historical Viewer
- **Toggles:** Daily, Weekly, Monthly, and Yearly views.
- **Yearly Aggregation:** Professional 1-row-per-year resampling.
- **Visuals:** Candlestick-style charts with integrated volume bars.

### 6. Technical Indicators Suite
- **Core Indicators:** RSI, EMA, MACD, ATR, PCR.
- **Narrative Intelligence:** AI-generated explanations of technical status.
